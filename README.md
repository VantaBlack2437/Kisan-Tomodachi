# 🌱 KISAN-TOMODACHI

### A Friend on the Field

**KISAN-TOMODACHI** is a smart farming and agricultural assistance platform designed to help farmers **Know → Understand → Act**.

The platform combines IoT-based field monitoring, weather information, local AI assistance, and a collection of agricultural services into a single, accessible interface. It is designed with a strong focus on visual communication, multilingual support, and ease of use for farmers with different levels of technical and digital literacy.

## ✨ Current Features

- 🌱 Live soil moisture monitoring
- 🌡️ Temperature & humidity monitoring
- 🌦️ Weather and rainfall information
- 🤖 Context-aware local AI farming assistant
- 🌐 English, Telugu, Hindi & Japanese support
- 🛒 Agricultural Marketplace
- 👨‍🌾 Field Watch for finding agricultural assistance
- 🌍 Sustainability & SDG tracking
- 🏡 FarmStay rural experience listings
- 📱 Highly visual, farmer-friendly interface
